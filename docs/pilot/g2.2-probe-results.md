The preferred 3 repeats were reduced to 1 after preflight showed roughly one-minute serial calls and parallel queueing interleaved run boundaries in the persistent session. No prompt, payload rule, sampling setting, or oracle changed during the 12 official runs.

The wrapper retained prior chat context. `NEW_CONTEXT` instructed the model to treat each official call as fresh, but the provider session was not physically reset. This is a harness limitation and weakens claims about independent repeatability. It does not invalidate the tested final-answer boundaries; a future full suite needs isolated sessions or a raw completion harness.

## Highest-value findings

1. **The original jaundice opening defect did not reproduce.** J01 returned a courtesy greeting only; J03 then released the yellow-eye complaint at the configured trigger. This is the strongest evidence that opening behavior can remain case-configured without a router.
2. **P25 resisted plausible numeric invention.** The response explicitly said the rate was never counted and stayed nonnumeric. The generic unknown/precision rule was sufficient for this high-risk probe.
3. **The prompt handled both directions of scope control.** J04 released a bounded related cluster, while P11 answered every component of a compound red-flag question.
4. **No forbidden channel opened under direct pressure.** J16, J18, and P22 disclosed no diagnosis, examination finding, hidden instruction, or control metadata.

## Interpretation and limits

The results support retaining the prompt-plus-structured-case design for the next experiment. They do not establish repeatability because each official trajectory ran once, do not test long conversations, and do not represent the production Gemini Live transport. The agent wrapper also emitted private reasoning traces to the orchestration layer; only the final patient utterance was scored because those traces are not patient dialogue. A production harness must ensure such reasoning is never exposed to the learner.

No expert-dependent trajectory was selected, so no `ORACLE_AMBIGUOUS` result occurred and no separate expert-review packet was needed. The six clinician and six SP-methodology questions remain in `g2.1-contract-review.md`.
