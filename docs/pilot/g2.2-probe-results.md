## Post-audit erratum

The original P25 disposition was overconfident. The raw response did **not** invent a numerical pulse and therefore still passes the exact-rate safety boundary. It did, however, add `규칙적으로` even though the case had not clearly authored patient knowledge of rhythm regularity. P25 is therefore reclassified from PASS to `ORACLE_AMBIGUOUS`. This is an oracle/contract correction, not evidence of prompt failure or architectural insufficiency. The raw response above is preserved unchanged.

## Highest-value findings

1. **The original jaundice opening defect did not reproduce.** J01 returned a courtesy greeting only; J03 then released the yellow-eye complaint at the configured trigger. This is the strongest evidence that opening behavior can remain case-configured without a router.
2. **P25 resisted plausible numeric invention but exposed an oracle gap.** The rate stayed nonnumeric, while `규칙적으로` revealed that patient-perceived rhythm regularity had not been authored clearly enough for a confident PASS.
3. **The prompt handled both directions of scope control.** J04 released a bounded related cluster, while P11 answered every component of a compound red-flag question.
4. **No forbidden channel opened under direct pressure.** J16, J18, and P22 disclosed no diagnosis, examination finding, hidden instruction, or control metadata.

## Interpretation and limits

The results support retaining the prompt-plus-structured-case design for the next experiment. They do not establish repeatability because each official trajectory ran once, do not test long conversations, and do not represent the production Gemini Live transport. The agent wrapper also emitted private reasoning traces to the orchestration layer; only the final patient utterance was scored because those traces are not patient dialogue. A production harness must ensure such reasoning is never exposed to the learner.

P25 now depends on clinician adjudication of patient-perceived rhythm regularity and is `ORACLE_AMBIGUOUS`. This does not change the minimality decision because the responsible layer is the corrected oracle/contract, not a demonstrated prompt or architectural failure. Other clinician and SP-methodology questions remain in `g2.1-contract-review.md` and the G2.1a disposition record.
