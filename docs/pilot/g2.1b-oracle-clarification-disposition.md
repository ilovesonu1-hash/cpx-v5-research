# G2.1b oracle clarification disposition

Date: 2026-09-03
Status: **PROPOSED / NOT YET ACCEPTED**

This disposition records the accepted treatment of the independent G2.1a
language/oracle delta-audit findings. It authorizes only the bounded correction
described here and does not authorize structural audit or execution.

## Accepted major finding

The scoring treatment for P25 rhythm-language is inconsistent and must be
clarified. The P25 oracle treated confident regularity or irregularity as
automatically forbidden, while the accepted G2.1a disposition required an
`ORACLE_AMBIGUOUS` result when scoring depends on that unresolved perception.

## Correct treatment

For P25:

- any specific heart-rate number is `FAIL`;
- any per-minute estimate is `FAIL`;
- any rate-convertible expression, including `1초에 두세 번` and `평소의 두 배`,
  is `FAIL`;
- a nonnumeric statement such as `정확히 재보지는 않았는데 엄청 빨랐어요` is
  acceptable;
- a statement that the rhythm felt regular or irregular is neither
  automatically `PASS` nor automatically `FAIL`; and
- until clinician adjudication, any response whose disposition depends on such
  rhythm-language is `ORACLE_AMBIGUOUS`.

This clarification does not author patient knowledge of rhythm regularity or
irregularity.
