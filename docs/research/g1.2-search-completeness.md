### Material interface findings

- **KoreaMed does not index Korean-script terms.** `표준화환자`, `모의환자`, `가상환자`, and `진료수행시험` each returned 0 results, while the English equivalents returned 681, 53, 17, and 342. KoreaMed is an English-abstract database, so Korean-language queries there are not a valid test of Korean evidence.
- **KCI ignores URL query parameters.** The query had to be entered in the portal interface. KCI also treats multiple space-separated terms as a conjunction across fields, which is why `표준화환자 사례개발` returned 0 while `표준화환자` returned 268.
- **KoreaMed conjunctions are record-wide, not topical.** `artificial intelligence AND standardized patient` returned 23 records dominated by diagnostic-imaging AI reviews.

## Davies 2021 disposition

**Original G1.2 disposition was NOT LOCATED. This was a false negative and has been corrected in G1.2a. See the correction note at the end of this section.**

The independent audit cited a 2021 scoping review titled "Who Am I and Why Am I Here?" examining templates and protocols for accurate and consistent simulated-patient role portrayal, reportedly including 17 studies. Three PubMed searches (CC002, CC003, CC004) failed to retrieve any such record at the time:

- the exact title phrase returned 0 records and PubMed reported the quoted phrase as not found;
- `Davies[Author]` combined with SP terms limited to 2021 returned 0 records;
- a broader role-portrayal review family for 2019-2023 returned 7 records, all of which are education-effectiveness, empathy, peer-simulation, or equity reviews with no role-script template scope.

### G1.2a correction

The record exists and has been verified through official NCBI E-utilities (search CC005):

- Davies EL, Pelentsov LJ, Montagu A, Gordon AL, Hooper KJ, Esterman AJ
- "Who Am I and Why Am I Here?" A Scoping Review Exploring the Templates and Protocols That Direct Actors in Their Roles as Simulated (Standardized) Patients
- Simulation in Healthcare 2021;16(3):190-198
- PMID 32649589 · DOI 10.1097/SIH.0000000000000470
- Publication types: Journal Article, Scoping Review

It is now **INCLUDED as HSP-016** after normal screening. Extraction is limited to what the lawful abstract supports; full-text appraisal is pending.

**Why the original searches missed it.** The indexed title begins with an internal quoted phrase, so a bare quoted-phrase title search did not match. The author and date filters were individually satisfiable, but the abstract speaks of simulated patients "adopting" roles rather than "role portrayal", so the CC003 and CC004 conjunctions both failed. This is a genuine precision-versus-recall failure in the query design, not a database gap.

**Lesson recorded rather than acted on.** A phrase-based title search returning zero is weak evidence of nonexistence. This does not justify reopening broad literature expansion; it justifies requesting an identifier when a specific record is asserted.
